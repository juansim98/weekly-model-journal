    Weekly routine

    1. Save the charts into the Charts folder as WEEK_PAIR.png — for example 2026-W31_USDJPY.png. Each card tells you the exact filename it is looking for.
    2. Click Add a week, fill the form, press Generate. Copy what appears.
    3. Open this file in Notepad. Near the top you will see ===== NEWEST WEEK GOES HERE =====. Paste directly under that line and save.
    4. Double-click publish.bat, then refresh. Same page, same links, one more week.
    5. One week on screen. ◀ Prev goes back in time, Next ▶ forward. New weeks always open first. To jump straight to a week, click the box on the right and start typing — W31, july and 2026 all narrow the list.
    6. Press a day to compare it. Click any square in a Mon–Fri strip and the page gathers every week holding a day worded exactly the same, newest-first — that is how you show the class the same setup repeating across months. Clear returns you to one week. Search, Pair and Bias narrow whatever is on screen.
    7. Charts open full screen. Click or tap any chart to enlarge it, tap again to zoom to full size and drag around. Esc or Close returns. This is the only way the charts are readable on a phone.
    8. Two links, one file. Give the class the plain address — it always opens on the newest week. Keep the same address with #edit on the end for yourself; that is the only place Present, Add a week and Labels appear. Bookmark both.
    9. The six models. The tab at the top opens The Six Weekly Flow Models as its own page — the six WFM models, what each looks like day by day, and the decision tree. Esc or Back to the record returns. Its address ends in #wfm, so you can give the class that link on its own. Inside a week's ranking, pressing a WFM badge opens this page at that model.
    10. Linking to one week. The address bar follows the week you are on, so to send the class a particular week — "this is the one to study" — just copy it while that week is on screen. It ends in #2026-W32 and will still open that same week months later, after newer ones have gone in.
    11. For class: open your #edit link, filter to one pair, then press Present. Arrow keys move, Esc leaves. The class sees a clean page with search and filters and nothing to break.

    Labels

    Your vocabulary, not a fixed list — every wording that appears on the page, with its colour. Nothing is grouped: each entry stands on its own, and pressing a day in a strip finds days with that exact wording. Rename, recolour, delete, or add your own. Used counts how many recorded days carry that label — a label in use cannot be deleted until you change those days first.

    ColourLabelUsed

    Apply now changes this screen so you can see the colours immediately — but it lasts until you refresh. To keep the change, press Generate and paste the result over the whole const LABELS = { ... }; block near the top of this file.
