    Weekly routine

    1. Save the charts into the Charts folder as WEEK_PAIR.png — for example 2026-W31_USDJPY.png. Each card tells you the exact filename it is looking for.
    2. Click Add a week, fill the form, press Generate. Copy what appears.
    3. Open this file in Notepad. Near the top you will see ===== NEWEST WEEK GOES HERE =====. Paste directly under that line and save.
    4. Double-click publish.bat, then refresh. Same page, same links, one more week.
    5. One week on screen. ◀ Prev goes back in time, Next ▶ forward. New weeks always open first. To jump straight to a week, click the box on the right and start typing — W31, july and 2026 all narrow the list.
    6. Two ways to filter. This week narrows the week you are on. All weeks hides the pager and gathers every week that matches — use it to show the class the same setup repeating across months.
    7. Charts open full screen. Click or tap any chart to enlarge it, tap again to zoom to full size and drag around. Esc or Close returns. This is the only way the charts are readable on a phone.
    8. Two links, one file. Give the class the plain address. Keep the same address with #edit on the end for yourself — that is the only place Present, Add a week and Labels appear. Bookmark both.
    9. For class: open your #edit link, filter to one pair, then press Present. Arrow keys move, Esc leaves. The class sees a clean page with search and filters and nothing to break.

    Labels

    Your vocabulary, not a fixed list. Rename, recolour, delete, or add your own. Used counts how many recorded days already carry that label — a label in use cannot be deleted until you change those days first.

    ColourLabelUsed
